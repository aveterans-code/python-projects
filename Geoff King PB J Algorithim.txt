Peanut Butter and Jelly Algorithm
1.	Start out with a desire for a sandwich.
2.	Look for someone to make a sandwich for me.
a.	After a search no-one is around so I need to do it myself.
3.	In the kitchen.
a.	Find a clean, large plate, butter knife, and paper towel. 
i.	Place these items on a clean counter surface where the sandwich will be made.
b.	Take the peanut butter out of the cupboard and place it on the counter.
c.	Take the raspberry jelly out of the fridge and place it on the counter.
d.	Find loaf of bread
i.	Open the bag and reach past the first heel of the loaf to grab two slices of bread.
ii.	Set the two bread slices on the plate.
iii.	Close bread bag
e.	Open jelly and set lid down out of the way on the counter.
i.	Pick up butter knife inserting the flat end into the jar to grab the desired amount of jelly.
ii.	Spread on slice of bread
iii.	Repeat process with peanut butter on the second, empty, slice of bread.
f.	Put the peanut butter covered slice onto the jelly slice with the peanut butter facing down toward the jelly.
i.	Cut sandwich if desired.
g.	Clean up dirty dishes.
i.	Place the dirty knife in the dishwasher.
h.	Place covers back on peanut butter and jelly jars.
i.	Put jars back where they came from.
j.	Double check I cleaned my mess.
k.	Enjoy sandwich
